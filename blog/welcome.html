Welcome to the GroupThat blog

If you’ve ever been the person floating $600 for a group trip while you wait for eight people to “get you back soon,” this one’s for you.

We built GroupThat because collecting money from a group is one of those small tasks that somehow eats an entire week. You send the group chat message. Two people pay right away. The rest go quiet. You send a gentle reminder. Then a less gentle one. Then you start doing math to figure out who still owes what — and by then you’ve become the unpaid accountant of your friend group.

There had to be a better way. So we made one.

What GroupThat does

GroupThat turns “chasing people” into a set-it-and-forget-it flow. You create a group, set what each person owes, and share a link. GroupThat handles the reminders, tracks who’s paid, and shows you exactly where things stand at a glance.

The goal is simple: you should never have to send “hey, just following up on that $20” ever again.

Whether it’s a group trip, a shared gift, a team lunch, or dues for a club, the pattern is the same — and GroupThat is built for all of it.

What to expect from this blog

We’ll use this space for a few things:

  • Product updates — new features, improvements, and the occasional behind-the-scenes.
  • Practical guides — how to split costs fairly, collect money without awkwardness, and organize groups that actually stay organized.
  • Stories — the good, the bad, and the “I can’t believe someone still hasn’t paid” of group finances.

Get started

GroupThat is available now on iOS and Android. If you’re tired of being the group treasurer, give it a try — and let us know what you’d like to read about next.
