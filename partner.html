The meetup rhythm

Be part of the plan, not an interruption.

Whether a group meets daily or weekly, every meetup follows a rhythm, and partners can show up when the group is deciding where to go and what to do.

  1. Invite

    The invite goes out

    GroupThat sends the next meetup's invite to every member automatically.

  2. Every day

    Members chat and RSVP

    People tap in, check who's coming, and sort out the details.

  3. Meetup

    The group meets up

    Reminders go out and the crew gets together in real life.

  4. Repeat

    It happens again

    Daily, a few times a week, or weekly: the cycle repeats, giving partners a steady, predictable audience.
